EMBER DUEL

A duel of cinders and gloom — first to snuff the other's flame wins.
You and the Gloom Warden each have 20 life. Every turn you gain an ember and draw a card. Play creatures onto the field, then strike with them — creatures attack the turn after they arrive. Spells hit at once. Click a ready creature, then click an enemy creature or the Warden himself. Keys: 1–5 play a card, Space ends your turn.
An original game — no network, no account, just cards.
